I started [Boxi Math](https://boxi-math.github.io/), a collection of free K–12 math videos in English and Chinese that show why math works, organized by stage rather than school year. The videos and text are CC BY 4.0, and the tools behind them are open source at [animind-ai](https://github.com/animind-ai). See [Education equity](/#equity-heading) on the home page.
