[FrontierCS 2](https://frontier-cs.org/), a benchmark I co-authored, is out on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29932). It tests whether AI agents can do computer science R&D: each of its 48 tasks removes a paper's key contribution from its released code, and an agent passes only by beating the original on hidden workloads. Watch the [one-minute video](/research/#frontiercs2) or see the [publication entry](/publications/#mang2026frontiercs2).
